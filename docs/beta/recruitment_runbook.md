# Closed Beta interest and recruitment runbook

Current state: **interest registration only; no Beta access and no real-data
intake.**

## Public interest registration

The GitHub `Beta interest registration` issue form may collect only broad role,
workflow, market, language and category preferences. It must not request names,
email addresses, schedules, employer/client identity, product files, source
documents, unpublished facts, personal data, secrets or production URLs.

Opening an issue does not create an account, reserve a place, authorize an
upload or start a free trial. Triage labels the issue and records only whether
the use case appears in scope.

## Private screening — blocked until a channel exists

After a dedicated private contact is selected and the relevant D5 decisions are
recorded, the maintainer may ask shortlisted applicants about data authority,
availability and agreements privately. Do not move private answers back into a
public issue.

## Invitation gate

No participant receives access or sends a product file until:

1. operator, provider, region, retention and contact disclosures are effective;
2. hosted identity/tenant denial, export/deletion and backup-purge tests pass;
3. model and total-cost limits, external alerts and emergency switches pass;
4. the participant preflight and any required agreement are complete;
5. a stable participant ID, project ID, allowed markets and expiry are recorded
   outside the public repository; and
6. the owner approves the exact candidate and cohort cap.

## Low-cost portfolio path

If these conditions cannot be met, keep the public Demo and interest list, use
fictional data for technical rehearsals, and report real-user Beta evidence as
not completed. AI review may supplement quality evidence but must not be
reported as a participant, independent professional review or adoption intent.
