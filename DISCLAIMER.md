# LocalizeFlow Disclaimer

Last updated: 2026-08-15

LocalizeFlow organizes evidence, runs deterministic checks, and supports a
human review workflow. A passing check means only that content passed the
project's frozen rules at that time. It does not establish truth, safety,
substantiation, legal compliance, or approval.

## No professional or platform approval

LocalizeFlow is not legal, regulatory, medical, safety, or financial advice; a
professional translation certification; scientific substantiation; or approval
from Google, TikTok, a marketplace, an advertising platform, a regulator, or
any public authority. Platform rules change and may depend on the account,
product category, campaign objective, destination page, audience, and
jurisdiction.

## Cosmetic and health-related claims

Cosmetic wording can imply health, treatment, performance, or guaranteed-result
claims even when those words are not stated directly. Disclaimers do not cure a
misleading overall impression. Users must verify both express and implied
claims, the quality and relevance of supporting evidence, labeling, required
qualifications, and the rules of every target market before publication.

Do not rely on the project for diagnosis, treatment, prevention, adverse-event
handling, product safety decisions, or regulated medical claims.

## AI and automation limitations

Model output and deterministic rules can omit context, reproduce bias,
mistranslate, hallucinate, or become outdated. Scores are decision support, not
proof. Human confirmation records a workflow action; it does not transfer or
remove the user's responsibility for final content.

Always conduct current factual, legal, platform, intellectual-property, brand,
safety, and target-language review before publication.
