# LocalizeFlow Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-15

This policy applies to the public deterministic Demo and use of the preview
software. It supplements, and does not replace, the Apache License 2.0 or
applicable law. No hosted free-trial service is currently active.

## Required rights and care

Only provide material you are authorized to use. You are responsible for
obtaining any necessary intellectual-property rights, confidentiality
permissions, privacy notices, consents, and other lawful basis for your inputs
and intended use. Use fictional or properly sanitized data when evaluating the
project.

## Prohibited use

Do not use LocalizeFlow to:

- violate law, regulation, court order, contract, or another person's rights;
- upload personal, confidential, customer, production, payment-card, health,
  authentication, or other sensitive data to the public Demo;
- create deceptive, unsubstantiated, counterfeit, infringing, impersonating,
  discriminatory, harassing, or exploitative content;
- represent cosmetic or consumer-product content as medical diagnosis,
  treatment, prevention, guaranteed results, or regulatory approval without
  competent authorization and substantiation;
- make high-impact decisions about health, employment, housing, education,
  credit, insurance, legal services, or access to essential services;
- exploit or endanger children, facilitate sexual abuse, or create intimate
  content without consent;
- distribute malware, phishing, credential theft, spam, or instructions whose
  primary purpose is to compromise systems or evade security controls;
- probe other users' data, cross tenant boundaries, bypass access controls,
  evade usage limits, overload a service, or conceal abusive automation; or
- claim that an output is approved by a platform, regulator, professional, or
  the LocalizeFlow project when it is not.

## Publication and human review

The project does not automatically publish to commerce or advertising
platforms. You must conduct current factual, legal, platform, brand, and target-
language review before using content externally. Automated checks and human
confirmation inside the workflow do not transfer that responsibility.

## Enforcement and reporting

A local operator is responsible for enforcing this policy in its installation.
Any future hosted operator may block inputs, suspend access, preserve evidence
where legally required, or report conduct when reasonably necessary to prevent
harm or comply with law; those procedures must be stated in the hosted terms
before launch.

Report security vulnerabilities through the private channel in `SECURITY.md`.
Do not include secrets, personal data, or exploit details in a public issue.
